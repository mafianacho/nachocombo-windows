# NachoCombo for Windows 0.2.0

Brings the Windows edition closer to Nacho Combo for macOS. Every new gadget starts **off**; your existing
gadgets, shortcuts and settings are kept.

## Fixed

- **The exe now runs on a clean Windows.** 0.1.0 needed the Visual C++ runtime (`VCRUNTIME140.dll`) and closed at
  once on a PC without it (error `0xC0000135`). The C runtime is now built into the exe.

## New

- **Nacho Pet** (off by default): a desktop character you can drag anywhere, with a chat window that follows it and
  can be resized. Sign in with the unified Nacho account (`auth.mafianacho.com`) in your browser (no administrator
  rights). You stay signed in when you restart. Approval and choice cards, a selection bar, and press-and-hold voice
  dictation.
  The Nacho account is the unified account, the same as Nacho Combo for macOS 0.12.0. An earlier Nacho account and its
  chats, memories and other data are not linked to it and do not move over.
- **Desktop actions for Nacho**: Nacho can use your gadgets only when you allow it. Anything that would send what is
  on your PC (clipboard, screen captures, files) asks every time, unless you turn on "always allow" for that gadget.
  Nacho has no actions that delete anything.
- **Calendar Clock** (off by default): a date icon in the tray, a month calendar and world clocks.
- **App Switch** (off by default): a window switcher, app shortcuts that open, raise or hide a program, and Hide Other
  Windows.
- **Mouse Tune** (off by default): wheel direction, scroll lines, pointer speed and precision. Your original settings
  come back when you turn it off.
- **Widget Board** (off by default): one window with a widget for each gadget, which you can rearrange and resize.
- **In-app updates**: checks GitHub Releases. It downloads and replaces the exe only after you confirm. Nothing is
  replaced on its own.

## Not checked yet

This release was tested with unit tests, CI and a Windows 10 virtual machine only. It has **not** been tried on a
physical PC. In particular:

- Quitting on a PC with a hardware GPU. A crash on quit while signed in to Nacho Pet was fixed and checked in the
  virtual machine only.
- The voice dictation level ring and dictation with a real microphone.
- Signing in all the way: the virtual machine check went as far as the unified login page opening from Nacho Pet. Entering
  an account and the return from the browser to the app were not tried.
- The App Switch shortcut (Ctrl+Alt+`) typed on a physical keyboard.

The exe is still not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn on first launch.
